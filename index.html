About Me

Hi, I’m Andy. I am of Spanish and Irish origin and grew up in the Netherlands in a bilingual household. Languages have shaped my life from an early age, and I am fluent in Spanish, English, Dutch, and French.

Growing up between cultures gave me a deep understanding of how language connects people. I bring cultural awareness, sensitivity, and curiosity into every lesson. My goal is not just to teach vocabulary or grammar — it’s to build confidence, communication skills, and genuine connection.

I am passionate about teaching in a way that feels relaxed, encouraging, and tailored to each student. Whether you're learning for travel, work, exams, or personal growth, I adapt each lesson to your pace and goals.
